Why Documentation Matters

Filing a Complaint Is Only the Beginning.

State insurance departments receive numerous complaints involving denied claims, delayed payments, disputed estimates, and claim-handling practices.

Simply stating that an insurance company acted unfairly may not fully explain what happened or why the conduct deserves regulatory review.

We believe a properly documented complaint should identify the facts, supporting evidence, and potentially applicable regulatory requirements.

Our Story

Why We Created Adjusters' Watch

Adjusters' Watch was created through experience working alongside professionals in the insurance industry who understand how insurance claims are investigated, evaluated, negotiated, and settled.

Through that experience, we have observed recurring claim-handling practices that can leave homeowners frustrated, financially vulnerable, and uncertain about their rights.

Repeated documentation requests, prolonged investigations, disputed repair estimates, and unclear coverage explanations can create significant obstacles for homeowners attempting to recover from property damage.

We created Adjusters' Watch because homeowners deserve a meaningful opportunity to have their concerns properly documented and reviewed.

We recognize that not every claim disagreement constitutes a regulatory violation. Our approach emphasizes factual accuracy, documentation, and identifying issues that may warrant further regulatory examination.

Service Information

Independent Consumer Complaint Assistance

Adjusters' Watch is an independent consumer complaint assistance service and is not affiliated with any state Department of Insurance.

Consumers may submit insurance complaints directly to their state insurance departments at no charge. Our administrative fees, when applicable, compensate us for optional complaint preparation and assistance services.

We do not guarantee regulatory findings, claim payments, coverage determinations, or other outcomes. A regulatory complaint does not necessarily suspend insurance policy deadlines or legal filing deadlines.

Our services are subject to applicable state laws, authorization requirements, and professional licensing restrictions. Adjusters' Watch is not presented as a law firm and does not provide legal representation.
